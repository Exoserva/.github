Exoserva brings customer communication and day-to-day service operations into one connected workspace. We build tools that help teams handle incoming requests, organize work, and follow a job from the first conversation through payment.

Our roots are in **Knock Knock Contractor LLC**. The product grew from the practical needs of a contracting business, and that operator perspective continues to guide our work.

### What we build

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Customer communication | AI-assisted voice, messaging, intake, and follow-up |
| Customer & job management | CRM, service requests, work orders, and customer portals |
| Scheduling & dispatch | Appointments, team availability, and field coordination |
| Estimates & finance | Estimates, invoices, payments, and connected business records |
| Field tools & integrations | Mobile workflows, reporting, and APIs |

### For developers and partners

This organization is the engineering home of Exoserva. Explore our [developer portal](https://exoserva.com/developers) and [API reference](https://exoserva.com/developers#endpoints) to learn about integration options.
